1. The 1%–2% Unit Bankroll Rule

The number one reason most bettors lose their bankroll is poor money management, not bad predictions. Professionals never bet random amounts based on gut feelings. Instead, they divide their total bankroll into fixed "units" — typically 1% to 2% of the total bankroll per bet.
