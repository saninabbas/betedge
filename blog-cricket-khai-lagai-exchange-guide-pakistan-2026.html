In Pakistani cricket culture, traditional betting is being rapidly replaced by Cricket Trading (Khai-Lagai). Instead of betting once and hoping for the best, smart punters trade odds dynamically ball-by-ball, locking in guaranteed profits (Green Book) before the 20th over is even completed.

1. What is Khai-Lagai (Back vs Lay)?

In a betting exchange model, you are not just betting against the house — you can act as the bettor or as the bookmaker:

  • Lagai (Back / Blue): You bet FOR an outcome (e.g. Backing Pakistan to win at 2.20). If Pakistan wins, you profit.
  • Khai (Lay / Pink): You bet AGAINST an outcome (e.g. Laying England at 1.25). If England loses or the match turns, you profit.

3. The Green Book Technique (Locking 100% Guaranteed Profit)

The biggest secret to successful cricket trading is creating a "Green Book" where you win regardless of which team wins the match:

  1. Step 1 (Lagai / Back): Bet PKR 1,000 on Team B at high odds of 2.80 at the start of their innings.
  2. Step 2 (Game Momentum Shifts): Team B scores 55 runs in the powerplay without losing a wicket. Their odds plummet to 1.35.
  3. Step 3 (Khai / Lay): Place a Lay bet of PKR 1,500 at 1.35.
  4. Step 4: You have covered your risk entirely and secured a guaranteed profit on both sides!
