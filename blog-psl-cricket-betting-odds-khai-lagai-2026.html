Understanding Khai-Lagai (Back and Lay)

Traditional sportsbooks only allow you to bet on a team to win (Lagai / Back). However, modern cricket exchanges and top betting apps also let you bet against an outcome (Khai / Lay):

  • Lagai (Back): Betting on a specific team to win (e.g. backing Lahore Qalandars or Islamabad United).
  • Khai (Lay): Acting as the bookmaker and betting that a team will lose.
