Cricket trading is revolutionizing sports betting across Pakistan. Unlike traditional single-bet wagers where you wait for the 40-over conclusion, cricket trading allows smart players to buy and sell odds (Back & Lay / Khai-Lagai) ball-by-ball to lock in guaranteed green profit before the match even finishes.

1. What is Cricket Trading vs Traditional Betting?

In traditional sports betting, you wager on an outcome and risk 100% of your stake on the final whistle. In Cricket Exchange Trading, odds fluctuate with every boundary, single, and wicket.

  • Back (Lagai): Betting that an event WILL happen (e.g. Pakistan to win at 2.10).
  • Lay (Khai): Betting that an event will NOT happen (acting like the bookmaker).
  • Hedging / Book-Making: Backing high and laying low to secure equal green profit on both teams regardless of who triumphs.

3. Proven Cricket Trading Strategies for 2026

A. Powerplay Momentum Scalping

During PSL and T20 matches, evaluate pitch conditions during overs 1–2. If swing and seam movement are evident, lay the opening batting partnership runs or back under-session scores before market odds adjust.

B. In-Play Crash & Recovery Hedging

When a favored team loses an early wicket, their match odds spike. Backing them at inflated odds and selling (laying) once a solid partnership stabilizes is the safest book-making model in subcontinental cricket.

C. Strict Bankroll & Margin Rules

Never commit more than 3% to 5% of your total balance on a single trade. Maintain sufficient liquidity to absorb sudden market swings.
