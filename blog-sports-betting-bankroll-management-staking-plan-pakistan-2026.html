Long-term sports betting success relies more heavily on mathematical discipline and capital preservation than picking winners. Applying a structured 1% to 3% unit staking strategy guarantees that even extended losing streaks will not wipe out your playing capital.

How to Implement Unit Staking

  1. Set an Isolated Bankroll: Allocate dedicated betting capital (e.g. PKR 30,000).
  2. Calculate 1 Unit (1% = PKR 300): Use 1 unit for standard confidence match wagers.
  3. Cap High-Confidence Bets at 3 Units (PKR 900): Never place all-in revenge bets after a loss.
