Stay in control

Responsible Gambling

Betting is entertainment, not income. Play safe, set limits and know when to stop.

Golden rules

  • • Only bet what you can afford to lose.
  • • Set deposit and time limits before you start.
  • • Never chase losses — walking away is winning.
  • • Don't bet under stress or influence.
  • • Take a break if it stops being fun.

Age requirement

You must be 18 years or older to gamble. All operators listed on BetEdge require age and identity verification.

Need help?

If gambling is affecting your life, reach out. Most betting sites offer self-exclusion and deposit-limit tools in account settings. Organisations such as GamCare and BeGambleAware provide free, confidential support.

Self-exclusion

Every reputable operator lets you set limits, take a timeout, or self-exclude. Use these tools any time you feel your betting is no longer under control.
