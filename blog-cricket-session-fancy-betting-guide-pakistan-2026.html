2. Understanding Session Rates: YES (Lagai) vs NO (Khai)

Session odds are formatted as a two-digit spread (for example: 45 - 47):

  • YES (Lagai / Over): You wager that the team will score 47 runs or more.
  • NO (Khai / Under): You wager that the team will score 44 runs or fewer.
  • The 2-Run Spread (45–46): This gap represents the bookmaker's commission margin.

3. Top 3 Pro Strategies for Session Traders

A. Powerplay In-Play Hedge

If the pitch has early dew or heavy swing, bookmakers often post high benchmark lines (e.g. 48+). Back the NO (Under) immediately before market odds plummet on early dot balls.

B. Lambi Middle-Over Scalp

During middle overs (10–14), slow spin bowling artificially lowers the Lambi total. T20 death overs (16–20) consistently produce 60+ runs. Back the YES (Over) on deflated middle-over Lambi lines for massive risk-reward value.
