Know before you bet

Wagering requirements explained

A welcome bonus almost always comes with a wagering (rollover) requirement. It's the number of times you must bet the bonus before you can withdraw winnings.

For example, a 35× requirement on a $100 bonus means you need to place $3,500 in bets before you become eligible to withdraw. Lower rollover = better value, so we always list it in each review.
