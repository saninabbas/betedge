2. What to Check Before Claiming a Betting Bonus

1. Wagering Requirements (Rollover)

Wagering requirement refers to the number of times you must bet the bonus amount before requesting a cashout. For example, a 5x wagering requirement on a PKR 1,000 bonus means placing PKR 5,000 worth of qualifying bets. Lower rollover (under 10x) is always best.

2. Minimum Odds Restrictions

Most sportsbooks require accumulator bets with minimum odds (e.g. 1.40 or higher per selection) for the wager to count towards bonus completion. Avoid platforms requiring unreasonably high minimum odds (over 2.00).
