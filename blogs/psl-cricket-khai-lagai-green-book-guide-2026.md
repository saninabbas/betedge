## Khai-Lagai Strategy Explained

* **Lagai (Back):** Favouring a team to win.
* **Khai (Lay):** Favouring a team to lose.

By entering early at high odds and laying off your stake when the match swings, you ensure your balance stays green no matter which side claims the trophy.
