Bet on Cricket

Fancy Betting (Session Runs) is the most thrilling form of live cricket trading in Pakistan. Unlike standard match winner wagers, session trading lets you trade on 6-Over Powerplay runs, 10-Over projections, and Lambi (20-Over totals) with rapid YES/NO odds.

Key Fancy Betting Markets

  • 6-Over Powerplay Session: Trade on projected runs during the initial fielding restriction overs.
  • Lambi (Innings Total): Predict whether the batting team will cross the final projected run threshold.
  • Player Runs Over/Under: Back top individual batsmen (e.g. 35+ runs).

Golden Rules of Session Trading

  1. Observe First 2 Overs: Always assess pitch bounce and swing before placing Powerplay wagers.
  2. Lock Green Book Profits: Hedge your positions as run rates fluctuate to guarantee profit on both outcomes.
